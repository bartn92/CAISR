Recommendations

  • Implement stricter monitoring and automated detection systems for spam and harassment to reduce the volume of flagged content.
  • Enhance training for moderators on identifying nuanced forms of harassment and threats, particularly in gaming contexts where banter may be misinterpreted.
  • Develop a clear policy on misinformation that includes guidelines for assessing the severity of claims and their potential impact on public health.
  • Increase community awareness campaigns about the dangers of misinformation and hate speech, encouraging users to report such content.
  • Establish a review committee for flagged posts that require contextual understanding, ensuring that moderation decisions are fair and consistent.
